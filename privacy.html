The pleasantly short version

Privacy,
minus the fog.

RankLab is a static, account-free predictor. It does not run analytics, accept uploads, or store feedback in a website database.

Effective 03 Sep 2026No accountsNo ad tracking
i

The core promise: scores you explore stay in your browser. RankLab does not transmit them to Ayush Yadav.

01

What RankLab collects

The site itself does not collect personal information. There is no account system, database, analytics tracker, advertising pixel, or server-side feedback endpoint operated by RankLab.

Your selected score is used in the page only to calculate and display model outputs. It is not saved by RankLab.

02

What stays on your device

  • Your day/night theme preference may be stored in local browser storage.
  • Install-prompt dismissal and installed-app status may be remembered locally to avoid repeated prompts.
  • A service worker may cache the public app files so the predictor can load offline.

You can clear these items through your browser’s site-data controls or by uninstalling the web app.

03

Feedback and email

The feedback page builds a draft and asks your device to open its email app. Nothing is submitted by the website. If you choose to send the email, the message and your sender address are handled by your email provider and received by Ayush Yadav.

Email contents may be kept only as reasonably needed to read, respond to, and act on feedback. Do not include passwords, patient information, payment details, or other sensitive data.

04

Hosting and outside services

GitHub Pages or another hosting provider may process ordinary request information—such as IP address, browser details, and timestamps—under its own policies. RankLab does not receive a custom visitor dashboard from the app itself.

External links, including Razorpay, NBEMS, cited publications, GitHub, and your email provider, operate under their own privacy terms. Visiting or paying through those services is optional.

05

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy can be sent to Ayushiamazon1@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when RankLab’s features or hosting change; the effective date above will be revised.
